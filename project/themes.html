Explore LEGO Themes

LEGO offers many different themes for builders of all interests and experience levels. Explore some popular themes below.

Popular Themes

LEGO Star Wars

Build spaceships, vehicles, and locations inspired by the Star Wars universe.

This theme is popular with fans who enjoy science fiction and collecting minifigures.

LEGO City

Create everyday scenes with buildings, vehicles, police stations, fire stations, and other city locations.

LEGO Technic

Explore detailed mechanical builds featuring moving parts, gears, and realistic vehicles.

LEGO Harry Potter

Recreate magical locations and scenes from the Harry Potter stories.

Choosing a LEGO Theme

When choosing a LEGO theme, consider your interests, available space, and budget. Some themes focus on display models, while others offer more interactive builds.
